Welcome, and thank you for your interest in this study!

In this experiment, you will view a series of images depicting everyday objects and rate their material appearance on three perceptual attributes: Glossiness, Translucency, and Texture.

Each image will be presented one at a time. You will use slider controls to indicate your perception of the object’s material properties.
